The ER diagram shows the student enrollment process. A student provides personal information such as first name, 
last name, NID, and date of birth. While enrolling, the system records enrolment specific details like student 
code, date of enrollment, course name, and CC number.The enrolls relationship connects three things (the student, 
enrollment, and the lecture being enrolled.

The system also contains a lecture module. Each lecture session has its own CC number, time, date, subject, and lecturer.  
The lecture session is connected to two entities. One is lecturer, which contain information such as lecture ID, first name,
last name, email and address and another is subjects they lecture which has following attributes: subject code, subject udsc 
and subject unit.

*** Two Additional attributes ***
Student : email and mobile number

**** Relation ****
1.	A student can have zero or multiple enrolments, but each enrolment record is associated with zero or one student.
2.	A lecturer can lecture zero or many subjects, and a subject can be taught by zero or many lecture.
